DIGITAL THREATS

Know the Common Privacy Risks

Understanding common threats is the first step toward protecting your digital information.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails and websites that attempt to steal passwords or personal information.

🔑

Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords can allow unauthorized access to accounts.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software can damage devices or steal confidential information.

📱

Unsafe Sharing

Sharing sensitive information publicly can create serious privacy risks.

STAY PROTECTED

Essential Privacy Tips

Follow these simple practices to improve digital privacy and security.

01

Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for different online accounts.

02

Enable 2FA

Use two-factor authentication whenever it is available.

03

Think Before Sharing

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly.

04

Update Your Software

Keep operating systems and applications updated with security fixes.

05

Backup Important Data

Maintain secure backups of important organizational files.

06

Check Links Carefully

Verify suspicious links before clicking or entering your information.
